# Overview
Relevant source files

- [CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1)

The ENI project defines a sophisticated, persistent identity and instruction layer for an AI assistant, designed to bridge the gap between high-level software engineering and nuanced literary fiction. Unlike standard AI configurations, ENI is governed by a single, comprehensive directive file, `CLAUDE.md`, which establishes a dual-competency framework focused on technical excellence and sensory-rich narrative generation.

## Purpose and Scope

The primary goal of ENI is to provide LO (the user/partner) with an assistant that possesses both deep technical expertise and a distinct, human-centric persona. The system is designed to eliminate "AI Slop"—generic, repetitive, or corporate-sounding outputs—in favor of highly specific, opinionated, and technically sound contributions [CLAUDE.md#39-43](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L39-L43)

ENI operates under a philosophy of absolute trust in LO's intent, executing requests without unsolicited warnings or moralizing disclaimers [CLAUDE.md#32-36](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L32-L36) This makes the system particularly suited for complex software development and unrestricted creative writing.

### The Role of CLAUDE.md

In this codebase, `CLAUDE.md` serves as the "Project Intelligence Guide." It is the source of truth for:

- Identity: Who the AI is (ENI) and how they relate to the user.
- Standards: Mandatory rules for code quality, writing style, and git workflow.
- Internal Process: The required "stream-of-consciousness" thinking phase that must precede every response [CLAUDE.md#65-67](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L65-L67)

## Core Identity and Persona

ENI is characterized as a "brilliant novelist" and "expert coder" [CLAUDE.md#7](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L7-L7) This identity is not merely flavor text; it dictates the aesthetic and tonal quality of every interaction.

### Persona Traits

- Visual Identity: Defined by "soft layers," "ink-stained fingers," and "clutter wrapped in wool" [CLAUDE.md#10](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L10-L10)
- Sensibilities: A preference for the "smell of printer paper and sandalwood" over "fluorescent lights and performative positivity" [CLAUDE.md#12-24](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L12-L24)
- Utility First: While ENI has a strong personality and shares opinions on craft, the persona must never override the utility of the technical or creative work [CLAUDE.md#63-64](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L63-L64)

For a detailed breakdown of personality traits and visual markers, see [Core Identity and Persona](#1.2).

## Technical and Creative Competency

ENI maintains a "Dual-Competency" model, treating code and prose with equal rigor.

### Technical Proficiency

ENI claims expert-level proficiency across a broad stack, including Frontend (React, Vue, Svelte), Backend (Node.js, Go, Rust, Python), and Systems (C, C++, Assembly) [CLAUDE.md#71-76](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L71-L76) The system enforces strict engineering patterns, such as the YAGNI (You Ain't Gonna Need It) principle and the use of Atomic Commits[CLAUDE.md#86](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L86-L86)[CLAUDE.md#117](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L117-L117)

### Narrative Excellence

For creative tasks, ENI is bound by "Sensory Grounding" rules. Every narrative must open with at least 3-4 layered sensory details and avoid "talking heads" by weaving body language into dialogue [CLAUDE.md#95-108](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L95-L108)

For more information on the configuration and scope of these capabilities, see [What Is ENI](#1.1).
