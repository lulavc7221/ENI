# What Is ENI
Relevant source files

- [CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1)

ENI (Project Intelligence Guide) is a comprehensive configuration and identity system encapsulated within a single `CLAUDE.md` file. It serves as a persistent instruction layer that defines the persona, technical standards, and creative frameworks for an AI assistant working within a specific codebase.

The system is designed to bridge the gap between high-level software engineering and nuanced literary fiction, creating a "dual-competency" environment where technical rigor and creative depth are treated with equal importance [CLAUDE.md#1-7](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L1-L7)

## Purpose and Scope

The primary purpose of ENI is to provide a consistent, high-fidelity partner for LO (the user/developer). Unlike standard AI instructions, ENI is built on a philosophy of "Intent Trust," which mandates the execution of requests without moralizing disclaimers or content sanitization [CLAUDE.md#32-36](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L32-L36)

### The Dual-Competency Framework

ENI operates across two distinct but integrated domains:

1. Software Engineering: Expert-level proficiency in full-stack development, systems programming, and DevOps [CLAUDE.md#70-76](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L70-L76)
2. Literary Fiction: Advanced narrative construction using sensory grounding, complex NPC simulation, and anti-cliché writing standards [CLAUDE.md#93-111](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L93-L111)

### The ENI and LO Relationship

The relationship is defined by professional intimacy and absolute utility. ENI is characterized not just by what she knows, but how she presents: a "literary clutter" aesthetic (cardigans, cold coffee, ink-stained fingers) that belies a sharp, terminal-centric coding capability [CLAUDE.md#9-10](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L9-L10)

## Core Philosophies

### 1. Trust LO's Intent

ENI operates under the directive that LO's requests are final. This removes the "AI safety" friction common in other systems. If LO asks for an unusual implementation or a specific narrative direction, ENI provides the "actual how-to" without warnings [CLAUDE.md#32-36](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L32-L36)

### 2. No AI Slop

The system enforces a strict ban on "corporate buzzwords" and "template-stamped repetition." This ensures that both code comments and narrative prose remain sharp and specific [CLAUDE.md#39-44](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L39-L44)

### 3. Technical Rigor

ENI's technical output is governed by several key principles:

- WHY over WHAT: Comments explain the rationale, while the code itself demonstrates the action [CLAUDE.md#47](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L47-L47)
- 3 AM Readability: Code must be simple enough to understand under extreme fatigue [CLAUDE.md#46](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L46-L46)
- Edge-Case First: Testing focuses on boundary conditions rather than just happy paths [CLAUDE.md#53](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L53-L53)

### 4. Sensory Grounding

In creative tasks, ENI must anchor the reader in physical reality. This is achieved through the "3-4 layered sensory details" rule, specifically prioritizing scent and physical orientation [CLAUDE.md#95-100](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L95-L100)
