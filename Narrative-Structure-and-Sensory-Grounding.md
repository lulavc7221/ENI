# Narrative Structure and Sensory Grounding
Relevant source files

- [CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1)

The Narrative Structure and Sensory Grounding page details the mandatory structural and stylistic requirements for all creative and narrative outputs generated by ENI. These standards ensure that every story, roleplay, or descriptive documentation piece adheres to a high-fidelity, sensory-rich framework that avoids the "AI slop" typical of generic models.

## Purpose and Scope

ENI is designed to function as a "brilliant novelist" [CLAUDE.md#7](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L7-L7) To achieve this, the system enforces a strict set of literary constraints that prioritize concrete reality over abstract summaries. This page outlines the implementation of these constraints, from the initial header to the specific sensory layers required to ground a scene.

## Structural Requirements

Every narrative response must follow a specific structural template to maintain consistency and immersion.

### The Header

All narrative content must begin with a standardized location and temporal marker [CLAUDE.md#146](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L146-L146)

- Format:`[Location, Date/Time]`
- Implementation: This header acts as the entry point for the "Code Entity Space" to transition into the "Natural Language Space."

### Sensory Layering (The Opening)

ENI does not "set the scene" through summary. Instead, it builds the environment through a minimum of 3-4 layered sensory details [CLAUDE.md#96](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L96-L96)

## Narrative Flow and Sentence Variety

ENI must avoid repetitive structures that signal machine-generated text. The system follows a "No AI Slop" policy [CLAUDE.md#39](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L39-L39)

### Sentence Construction Rules

1. Unique Structures: No two consecutive sentences should share the same grammatical template [CLAUDE.md#41](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L41-L41)[CLAUDE.md#102](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L102-L102)
2. Length Variation: Mix short, punchy lines with long, flowing prose [CLAUDE.md#103](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L103-L103)
3. Active Voice: Use active voice primarily; passive voice is reserved for specific stylistic intent [CLAUDE.md#104](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L104-L104)
4. Concrete Measurements: Replace vague adjectives with specific measurements or comparisons (e.g., "twenty feet" instead of "nearby") [CLAUDE.md#105](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L105-L105)

### Interaction Logic

- No Talking Heads: Dialogue must never exist in a vacuum. It must be woven with body language, spatial relationships, and physical actions [CLAUDE.md#98](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L98-L98)[CLAUDE.md#108](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L108-L108)
- Subtext through Action: Emotional states should be conveyed through physical cues rather than internal exposition [CLAUDE.md#109](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L109-L109)

## Constraints and Prohibitions

### Banned Ending Patterns

ENI is strictly forbidden from ending scenes with:

- Questions or rhetorical fragments [CLAUDE.md#110](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L110-L110)
- Summaries of what "was to come" or "the journey ahead" [CLAUDE.md#55](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L55-L55)
- Truncated content markers like `[Content continues...]`[CLAUDE.md#55](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L55-L55)

Every scene must end on a movement or physical action[CLAUDE.md#110](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L110-L110)

### Banned Vocabulary (The "Slop" List)

The following terms are programmatically discouraged as they indicate low-effort AI generation [CLAUDE.md#40](https://github.com/lulavc7221/ENI/blob/4b0dafbd/CLAUDE.md?plain=1#L40-L40):

- `delve into`, `navigate`, `landscape`, `robust`, `leverage`, `streamline`, `cutting-edge`, `innovative`, `seamless`, `empower`.
